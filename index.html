Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across major neighborhoods in Bengaluru and Coimbatore. You can enter your 6-digit pincode on our Delivery Coverage page to check instant availability.

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is FREE on orders above ₹500. Express 30-minute delivery is FREE on orders above ₹799 (otherwise a nominal ₹49 fee applies).

Can I schedule a delivery?

Yes! During checkout, you can pick any convenient 2-hour window between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM up to 7 days in advance.

Can I pick up my order from a store?

Yes! Curbside pickup is 100% free with no minimum order requirement. Your groceries will be packed and ready when you arrive.

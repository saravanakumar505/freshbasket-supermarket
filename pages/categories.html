How are products organized?

Our items are grouped into 12 main supermarket aisles (Fresh Produce, Dairy, Bakery, Pantry, etc.) for easy browsing.

Can I search within a specific department?

Yes! You can use the search bar at the top of the categories page or shop catalog to filter by keyword.

Are fresh products restocked daily?

Absolutely! Our farm produce and bakery items arrive fresh every morning at 6:00 AM.

Can I filter products by price or brand?

Yes! The main Shop page features comprehensive sidebar filters for brand, price range, organic certification, and customer ratings.

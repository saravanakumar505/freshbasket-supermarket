Our Story

From a Single Grocery Store to a Next-Gen Platform

Founded in 2010 in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, FreshBasket started as a humble neighborhood grocery store dedicated to sourcing fresh vegetables directly from regional farmers. Over the past 15+ years, we have grown into a multi-store supermarket network serving over 50,000+ local families every week.

In 2018, we embraced e-commerce, introducing 30-minute doorstep express delivery, automated cold-chain logistics, and saved weekly list reordering while preserving our core commitment: genuine supermarket pricing, zero synthetic chemical spray, and warm community service.

Our Mission

Make everyday grocery shopping easier, faster, and healthier for every household through farm-fresh sourcing, transparent pricing, and effortless digital convenience.

Our Vision

Fresh food, fair prices, and stronger communities. To become the most trusted neighborhood supermarket brand across the nation.

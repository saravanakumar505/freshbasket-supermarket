Local Supermarket FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do your organic fruits and vegetables come from?

Our produce is harvested every morning directly from certified local organic farms in Indiranagar and Coorg orchards.

What happens if a product is out of stock?

Our store team will call or message you with a fresh alternative before packing your order.

How does curbside pickup work?

Park in our designated pickup bay, show your order code, and our staff will place your packed bags in your vehicle.

Is there a minimum order amount for delivery?

There is no minimum order requirement! Orders above ₹500 qualify for FREE standard delivery.
